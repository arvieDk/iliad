How to play

Win the gods, win the war

  1. Toss the coin. The winner moves first. Each side starts with two tiles in the center of the board and two in hand.
  2. Place a tile. Put one tile on an empty space of your color that touches another tile. You then draw back up to two.
  3. Use its power, if you like. Tiles 1 to 4 have an optional effect. 5 is simply strong, and Dolos copies its neighbors.
  4. Win the lines. When a row or column is full, the higher total wins it. The winner takes one token from its ends; the loser gets the other. A tie goes to the player whose turn it is.
  5. Count the spoils. At the end, a side with all five gods wins (four gods plus both marriages also counts). Otherwise the most points win: only your best token of each god scores, plus the +10 and negative tokens.

Ties are broken by lower god tokens, then the best Zeus, then the best Poseidon. Hover or focus any tile or token in the game to read what it does. Press Escape to put back a picked tile or token.
